Privacy Policy for Sweeping Clock

Last Updated: October 11, 2026 | Hosted URL: https://alok0248.github.io/privacy/

Sweeping Clock ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application, Sweeping Clock (including Live Wallpaper, Home Screen Widgets, and Screen Saver features), handles your information.

1. Information Collection and Use

Sweeping Clock does NOT collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data.

  • No Personal Information: We do not ask for, collect, or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or device identifiers.
  • No Background Tracking: The app does not track your online activity or physical location.
  • Local Preference Data: User customizations (such as selected clock themes, clock hand styles, custom colors, and preferred world clock timezones) are stored strictly on your local device using Android's standard local preference storage (DataStore). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or third parties.

2. Permissions Required and Purpose

Sweeping Clock uses standard Android system permissions required solely for clock functionality:

  • SET_WALLPAPER / SET_WALLPAPER_COMPONENT: Required to set and render the live sweeping analog clock wallpaper on your home and lock screens.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Required to automatically resume live clock widget updates after your device reboots.
  • WAKE_LOCK: Used temporarily by Android Screen Saver / Dream Service to keep the display active while docked in desk clock mode.
  • INTERNET: Standard platform framework system permission used exclusively for system library compatibility. No telemetry, analytics, or user data is sent over the network.

3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

Sweeping Clock does NOT integrate any third-party advertising SDKs, tracking frameworks, or remote analytics tools (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or AdMob).

4. Children’s Privacy

Sweeping Clock complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Family Policy requirements. Our app does not target or collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or local legal equivalent).

5. Security

Because all user settings remain 100% local on your Android device, your information is protected by Android’s native sandboxing security model.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted within this document with an updated revision date.
